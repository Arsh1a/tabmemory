## [0.1.3] — 2026-05-07

### Added

- PDF indexing: PDFs are now extracted and semantically indexed alongside regular pages
- Detects PDFs by `Content-Type: application/pdf` response header, so URLs that serve PDFs without a `.pdf` extension (e.g. IEEE stamp links) are handled correctly
